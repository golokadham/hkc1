Welcome to our Krishna conscious family

We are a growing family of devotees, friends, and well-wishers in Bloomington-Normal, Illinois, sharing the eternal wisdom of the Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagavatam, the bliss of kirtan, and the sacred practice of chanting the holy names of Krishna. Inspired by the teachings of Srila Prabhupada and the worldwide ISKCON movement, we gather each week for hari-katha, spiritual discussion, festivals, seva, and prasadam. Whether you are newly curious, sincerely seeking, or simply looking for a peaceful place to reconnect, you are always welcome here.
